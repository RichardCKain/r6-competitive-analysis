# Rainbow Six Siege · competitive analysis (Sep 2025 – Sep 2026)

Public, self-contained visualisations comparing **Rainbow Six Siege** to Call of Duty, Overwatch, Valorant, Apex Legends and Marvel Rivals across Steam, Twitch, esports, Google Trends and press.

Live: https://richardckain.github.io/r6-competitive-analysis/

R6 is the hero series in every chart (consistent orange highlight; rivals muted).

## Caveats (short)
- **Steam = PC Steam only.** No consoles / Ubisoft Connect / Battle.net / EA app / Riot. **Valorant is not on Steam.**
- **Twitch only** (SullyGnome); CoD is the sum of four categories.
- **Press tone** and timeline tones are hand-coded samples.
- **Esports Charts** free-tier limits; some peaks from secondary citations; Chinese platforms excluded.
- Data window Sep 2025 – Sep 2026; retrieved 2026-10-08.

Phase 2 (X / Twitter samples) will slot into the hub when collection finishes.
